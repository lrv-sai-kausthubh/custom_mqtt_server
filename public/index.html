How it works: every station is an MQTT topic filter. + matches one level and # matches every level below it. For example, home/kitchen/# receives every message under the kitchen, while home/+/status receives one status topic per room. Tuning submits an actual subscribe/unsubscribe to your Mosquitto broker.
